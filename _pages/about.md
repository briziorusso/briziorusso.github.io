I have been building AI models for the last ten years. Models to automate decisions, models to comply with regulations, models to forecast outcomes and make better decisions. 

My research interests are towards Causality, Computational Argumentation and Explainable AI, with a focus towards knowledge discovery and human-AI integration for high-stakes decision-making.

I have worked for 6 years as a consultant, delivering modelling solutions for different problems within Credit Risk, and leaving my job as Head of Data Science for [4most Europe](https://www.4-most.co.uk/machine-learning) in September 2020 to pursue a PhD in Computer Science. 

I obtained my PhD from [Imperial College London](https://www.imperial.ac.uk) in March 2025, funded by UKRI within the Safe and Trusted AI Centre for Doctoral Training [(STAI)](https://safeandtrustedai.org).

Currently, I am a Research Associate in the [Computational Logic and Argumentation group](https://clarg.doc.ic.ac.uk/), [Department of Computing](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/computing) at [Imperial College London](https://www.imperial.ac.uk). 

Check out my [CV](/cv/) to know more or get in touch via [email](mailto:brizio.russo@gmail.com) or [Linkedin](https://www.linkedin.com/in/fabriziorusso).

Additionally, I help organising the [Explainable AI Seminars @ Imperial](http://xaiseminars.doc.ic.ac.uk/), a great forum to learn and stay up to date with all things XAI.
